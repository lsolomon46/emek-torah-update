Torah Studies Update · Fall 2026

The year is off to a tremendous start.

Every few months we will share a short update, so you can see what is happening in Torah Studies across the school. Here is a quick look at what we are building this year.

A Map for Every Grade

Every grade has a Curriculum Map that lays out, week by week, what is taught in Kriah, Chumash, Halacha, Yedios Klaliot and Parsha. Teachers plan together on it, and we use it to make sure every class stays on track.

A School-Wide Shabbos Curriculum

We built clear Shabbos standards for every grade, Pre-1st through 8th, and teachers follow a week-by-week lesson plan. The unit ends with a school-wide assessment. We then review the results and gather teacher feedback to make the next unit even stronger.

Shabbos→Chanukah→Brachos

Every Child Assessed

Every child in grades 2–8 reads one-on-one with a teacher using our new Kriah (Hebrew reading) app, which shows exactly which reading skills are strong and which need practice. Each class then follows an action plan, and we reassess in the winter and spring to track every child's growth.

Practical and Active Learning

Interactive Chumash lessons on the smartboard, review games before each Yom Tov, and digital tools that help teachers use assessment data to plan their next steps.
