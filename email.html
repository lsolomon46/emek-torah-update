📘  A Curriculum Map for Every Grade

Every grade has a Curriculum Map that lays out, week by week, what is taught in Kriah, Chumash, Halacha, Yedios Klaliot and Parsha. Teachers plan together on it, and we use it to make sure every class stays on track.

A real page from our 3rd grade Curriculum Map

🕯️  A School-Wide Shabbos Curriculum

This fall, every grade is learning Shabbos. We built clear standards for each grade level, and teachers follow a week-by-week lesson plan. The unit ends with a school-wide assessment. We will then review the results and gather feedback from our teachers to make the next unit even stronger.

Coming up:  Shabbos → Chanukah → Brachos

✅  Kriah: Every Child Assessed

Every child in grades 2–8 reads one-on-one with a teacher using our new Kriah (Hebrew reading) assessment app, which shows exactly which reading skills are strong and which need more practice. Each class then follows an action plan, and we reassess in the winter and again in the spring to track every child's growth.

Grade 2: Decoding
Children read letters, vowels and words, level by level, with no timer. The goal is accuracy.		 Grades 3–8: Fluency
A word list, then a familiar and a new passage for one minute each. We measure accuracy and words read correctly per minute.

The research behind it: one-minute reading checks and running records are among the most widely used and studied ways to measure reading growth.

🎮  Practical and Active Learning

Interactive Chumash lessons on the smartboard, review games before each Yom Tov, and digital tools that help our teachers use assessment data to plan their next steps.

📈  A Focus on Data and Growth

This year we are piloting a skills-growth program in upper elementary that will be moving into middle school. Through individualized assessments, we follow each child's growth in core reading, writing and Chumash skills, such as gematria, shorashim, prefixes and suffixes, reading Rashi, and Ksiva (Hebrew writing). Much of it is aligned to the JSAT standards.

Our goal: a growth portfolio for every child, similar to MAP testing, bringing together Kriah, Chumash and, for middle school boys, Gemara. It will give a fuller picture of where each child is holding and where there is room to grow over time.
